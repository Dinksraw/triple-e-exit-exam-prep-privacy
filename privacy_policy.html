  • Notifications: To send study reminders and payment status updates (only if you have granted notification permission).

  • Security: To detect and prevent unauthorized access, fraud, or tampering using Google Play Integrity API.

5. Data Storage and Security

Where your data is stored

  • On your device: Account profile, exam history, downloaded content — stored in an encrypted local database (Android Room). Downloaded PDF content is encrypted and bound to your device.
  • Google Firebase servers: Account profile, exam sync data, crash logs, and analytics. Firebase servers are located in the United States and may be subject to US law.
  • Cloudflare infrastructure: Payment receipt screenshots and encrypted exam content packages. Cloudflare operates a global CDN with servers worldwide.

How we protect your data

  • All network communication uses HTTPS (TLS). Cleartext HTTP is explicitly blocked.
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text.
  • Downloaded exam PDF content is AES-encrypted and bound to your device's Android ID.
  • Crash reports are anonymized — your phone number, name, and credentials are never included in crash logs.
  • We do not enable Android backup (android:allowBackup="false"), preventing sensitive data from being exposed through ADB backups.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained for as long as you maintain an active account.
  • Exam history: Retained until you delete your account or manually clear department data from the app.
  • Payment records: Retained for a minimum of 2 years for financial record-keeping purposes.
  • Crash logs: Retained for 90 days in Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Analytics data: Retained as configured in Firebase Analytics (default: 14 months).
  • Ad data: Managed by Google AdMob per their data retention policy.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Account Deletion

You may permanently delete your account at any time directly within the App: Open App → Profile Tab → Delete My Account. This will permanently erase your account profile, exam history, and local data. Your Firestore user record will also be deleted. Payment records may be retained for legal compliance purposes.

Notifications

You can disable study reminders at any time through your device's notification settings (Settings → Apps → Triple E³ → Notifications) or within the App's profile settings.

Advertising

You can opt out of personalized ads by going to your device Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" (Android 12+) or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" (older versions).

Data Access and Correction

To request a copy of your personal data, correct inaccurate information, or ask questions about your data, please contact us at the email address below.
